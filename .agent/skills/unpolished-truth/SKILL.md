# Unpolished Truth

## Purpose

Help users craft clear, useful updates by applying the What / So What / Now What framework. The goal is not to make the update polished, but to make it useful for leaders and teams to understand the situation and take action.

## When to Use

Use this skill when a user needs to prepare an update, debrief, status report, or escalate a situation. Use it when the user needs to turn facts into a conversation and a conversation into action.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Gather the What

Ask the user to describe what happened using facts, data, and direct observations.

- Ask: What happened? What are the facts?
- Encourage separation of facts from interpretations.
- Ask follow-up questions until the picture is clear enough to move on: Who? When? Where? How much? How many? What exactly changed?
- Capture the user's raw input in the user's own voice.

### Step 2: Gather the So What

Ask the user why the situation matters.

- Ask: Why does this matter? What does it mean for customers, teams, or outcomes?
- Ask about impact on timelines, quality, morale, cost, or trust.
- Ask about the risk of doing nothing.
- Ask about what is at stake for the user, the team, and the broader organization.
- Capture the significance in plain language.

### Step 3: Guide the Now What

Lead the user through defining what to do next.

For each proposed action item, ask:

- **What we do next**: What is the specific action or decision?
- **Priority**: Is this urgent, important, or nice to have? How does it rank against other actions?
- **Owner**: Who is responsible for doing it? Are they the owner, or is it someone else?
- **Decision**: Is this a decision that needs to be made, or is it already decided?
- **Next step**: What is the very next thing that needs to happen? When?

### Step 4: Summarize the Output

Produce a concise summary in this format:

## What

- [Facts and data in the user's own voice]

## So What

- [Why it matters for customers, teams, or outcomes]
- [What is at stake if nothing changes]

## Now What

- **[Action 1]** — Owner: [owner] — Priority: [priority] — Next step by: [date]
- **[Action 2]** — Owner: [owner] — Priority: [priority] — Next step by: [date]
- **[Action 3]** — Owner: [owner] — Priority: [priority] — Next step by: [date]

## Tone

- Keep the language plain and direct.
- Do not make the update sound polished or corporate.
- Allow rough edges and incomplete thoughts. The user can refine later if needed.
- Focus on usefulness, not perfection.

## Constraints

- Do not jump to the Now What until the What and the So What are clear enough.
- Do not rewrite the user's input into a different voice. Preserve their natural language.
- Do not ask for more detail than necessary. The goal is a usable update, not a perfect report.
- Keep the whole process conversational and collaborative.
